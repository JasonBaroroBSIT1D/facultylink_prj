About FacultyLink

Faculty Self-Assessment and Rank Simulation System

FacultyLink helps faculty members organize academic evidence, review their KRA accomplishments, and explore simulated academic rank outcomes. The platform supports DBM–CHED aligned preparation workflows for higher education institutions.

FacultyLink provides self-assessment estimates and rank simulations for reference only. Results do not replace official faculty performance evaluations, promotion approvals, or academic rank reclassification decisions.

CHED building imagery is used for visual context only and does not imply CHED endorsement.
