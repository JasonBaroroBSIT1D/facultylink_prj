About FacultyLink

Faculty Self-Assessment and Rank Simulation System

FacultyLink is designed to support a structured and transparent faculty self-assessment process. It brings together document submission, evidence validation, reviewer verification, and KRA-based evaluation to help faculty members prepare and monitor their academic accomplishments.
